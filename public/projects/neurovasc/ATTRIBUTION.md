# NeuroVasc application captures

Actual NeuroVasc UI rendering of an annotated TopCoW/IXI MRA example. Underlying MRA: IXI Dataset, Brain Development, https://brain-development.org/ixi-dataset/. Circle-of-Willis annotations: TopCoW Challenge Organizers, https://zenodo.org/records/15692630. TopCoW publication: Yang K, Musio F, Ma Y, et al. The TopCoW Challenge — Topology-Aware Circle of Willis Segmentation for CT and MR Angiography. NEJM AI. 2026;3(8). https://doi.org/10.1056/AIdbp2500994.

Dataset-derived captures: CC BY-SA 3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/. Modifications consist of segmentation rendering, highlighting, chart and UI overlays by NeuroVasc. No raw scans or segmentation files are redistributed. The screenshots illustrate software behavior, not clinical validation.
